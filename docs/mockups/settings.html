Design notes

  • Language is personal: each member reads Kept in their own. It starts from the browser's language at sign-up. Each language is named in itself (English, Русский). Changing it reloads the page in that language; My purchases in Russian shows the result.
  • Settings opens from the user menu, on Profile. Tabs: Profile, Household (with its own row: Members, Categories, People & things, Name & currency), Assistants & keys, Assistant notes. The tab is in the URL.
  • Name through a new name field on PATCH /api/v1/setup. The time zone is the household's, under Household → General.
