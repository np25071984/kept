Design notes

  • Options 1–4 are a light mark on the brand green; 5–7 put a gold coin on it, warm against the cool green, so they read on light and dark tabs alike.
  • The test that matters is 16px: thin lines and small text disappear there, so each mark is two or three bold shapes.
  • The chosen one becomes favicon.svg plus PNGs for the app icon (180, 192, 512) and the header logo beside "Kept".
