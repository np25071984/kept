Design notes

  • Time zone is a search picker, as in holdmytrack: zones grouped by region and sorted by today's GMT offset, west to east, labelled "(GMT−04:00) New York". Typing filters by place, region or IANA id, ignoring case and accents ("sao" finds São Paulo); ↑/↓ and Enter pick, Escape closes. Prefilled from the browser. "It's 2:14 PM there" follows the zone picked, so a wrong choice shows at once.
  • Shown only after the code, so nobody learns whether an email has an account.
  • The form shows at once: an email with no account and no invite has nothing else to do here. The invite hint sits above the form, so someone expecting to join reads it before typing.
  • Currency and time zone come prefilled from the browser; the two names are typed.
  • The same form shows when the sign-in came from an assistant; it then continues to consent and the assistant's first conversation does the rest of setup.
  • Four fields only: starting categories are the fixed seed, and people & things come from the first conversation (VISION.md §3.6).
  • Replaces the create-household command for new users.
