Design notes

  • Opened from the Reports menu in the header; Mine is the other choice. Two parts. The top never changes with the filter: the per-month chart, whose header carries October so far, its comparison, the 12-month average and the month's projection. Breakdown follows one dropdown. Its three folder tabs, attached to the chart and never scrolling (they tighten on a phone), are pages of their own (the tab is in the URL, and the period carries over): By category, By people & things, By merchant. Each is the same chart: the biggest change, bars with a tick for the same days of the last period, and the total, which the bars add up to.
  • The dropdown's dates show beside it. "Custom…" adds From and To fields in their place. The choice is in the URL.
  • The same page as Reports · Mine over scope=household: every member's non-private purchases.
  • No chart or table splits by member; that rule is the ledger's, so the API doesn't offer it either (VISION.md §4.A).
  • Private purchases are left out entirely, By merchant included.
  • No watched groups and no caps here: both are personal, so they are in Reports · Mine.
  • By merchant names only merchants with purchases from at least two members, or a household-wide total above a floor; the rest go into "Other merchants", so one member's habit isn't singled out.
