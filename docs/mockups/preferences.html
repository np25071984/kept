Design notes

  • Opened from the user menu. Personal (VISION.md §3.3; §4.A "Caps").
  • Each watched group shows this month's amount found, how much of the spending where it's bought was checked, and the estimate for the rest (VISION.md §4.A, "Coverage"); low coverage reads as "about". Older months: ask the assistant.
  • "Watch something" picks a category or names an item; "Stop watching" removes it, and purchases already logged keep their checked groups.
  • Caps are set here or in chat. Progress counts your own purchases, private ones included; "close" appears from 80%. The form shows after "Set a cap" and, filled in, after "Edit". "Remove" deletes the cap at once.
