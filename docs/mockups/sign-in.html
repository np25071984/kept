Sign in

New to Kept, or invited by someone? Sign in with your email all the same.

Design notes

  • One way in: everyone signs in with an email and a code, and what happens next depends on the account (VISION.md §3.6, "One way in").
  • An account goes straight in; an invite gets the join step; no account and no invite gets "Create a household" (see After the code).
  • Every email gets a code, so the page reveals nothing about who has an account; codes are rate-limited per email and per address.
  • Every sign-in asks for a code (prompt=login), so signing out on a shared computer holds.
