Design notes

  • Members and Settings are new and owner-only; members see the list without the buttons.
  • Removing a member keeps their purchases in the ledger but out of household totals from then on; they lose access at once (grants and keys revoked). New endpoints: GET/DELETE /api/v1/members, GET /api/v1/invites.
  • The last owner can't be removed or demoted.
  • "Linked categories" is renamed "Ask about it for", which is what the link does.
  • Rules are only deleted here; new ones come from conversation, where the assistant can confirm the wording.
