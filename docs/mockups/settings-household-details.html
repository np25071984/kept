Design notes

  • One time zone for the household (VISION.md §3.6): owners change it, members see it read-only. It is a search picker: zones grouped by region and sorted by today's GMT offset, west to east, labelled "(GMT−04:00) New York". Typing filters by place, region or IANA id, ignoring case and accents ("sao" finds São Paulo); ↑/↓ and Enter pick, Escape closes. "It's 2:14 PM there" follows the zone picked.
  • Owners only; members see the values read-only.
  • Saved through PATCH /api/v1/setup.
