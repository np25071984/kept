Design notes

  • All personal (VISION.md §4.A, "Notes", "Caps"; §3.3).
  • Watched groups come first because they shape the questions most. "Watch something" picks a category or names an item; "Stop watching" removes it, and purchases already logged keep their checked groups.
  • Caps are set here or in chat. Progress counts your own purchases, private ones included; "close" appears from 80%. The form shows after "Set a cap" and, filled in, after "Edit". "Remove" deletes the cap at once; "Cap eating out at $250" in chat sets it again.
  • Notes are plain text, newest first, with the date saved. Assistants make most of them in conversation and write any time as a date; "Add a note" and "Edit" open a text box.
