Design notes

  • New page over the existing /api/v1/repeats endpoints; recurring bills are made from a purchase ("Make it recurring") or by telling the assistant.
  • Sorted by next date; paused ones last. The header sums what recurring bills log this calendar month (October: rent, Verizon and the half-yearly Geico) and how much of it is still to come: on October 5 it read "October: $2,195.00 · $955.00 still to come" (Geico and Verizon not yet logged), "all logged" once it all is.
  • Row actions are icons with tooltips: pause (play to resume), edit, delete. Delete ends the schedule and removes it from the list; it asks in place: "Delete the recurring Verizon bill? Purchases already logged stay."
  • The amount follows the latest real bill, so "Last" shows where it came from.
