Design notes

  • My purchases for a member whose Language is Русский, in a household that set up its categories and people & things in Russian. Everything else on the page follows the English one.
  • Translated: every label, button, placeholder, tooltip and screen-reader label. Not translated: what the household typed (categories, people & things, merchants, items, notes); a Russian household simply typed them in Russian.
  • Dates and money follow the language through Intl: "ср, 28 окт.", "2 645,20 $". The currency is still the household's.
  • Russian runs about 20–30% longer than English: buttons and tabs must not have fixed widths, and labels wrap rather than cut.
  • Plurals come from the message catalogue, not string building: 1 покупка, 2 покупки, 12 покупок.
