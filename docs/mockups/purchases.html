Design notes

  • Grouped by month with the month's total; the count and total at the top cover every match, not just what's loaded.
  • Paging: 50 at a time, "Load older" fetches the next page by date cursor (new before parameter); filters live in the URL.
  • Dashed tags are sources; a purchase confirmed by a statement shows both chat and statement.
  • Search covers merchant and item names in one box.
  • New API filters: source, private, q, before.
  • A purchase logged by a recurring bill has a "↻ recurring" tag linking to it on Recurring. The row itself opens the purchase: its link is the merchant name, stretched over the row, so the tag can be a link of its own.
  • Category and For pick several: a checkbox list opens under the field, and the field shows the first pick and how many more ("Groceries +1"). Ticking a parent includes its subcategories, shown ticked and greyed. "Nobody in particular" finds purchases with no person or thing. The API takes repeated category and cost_center parameters.
