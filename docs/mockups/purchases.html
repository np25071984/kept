Design notes

  • Grouped by month with the month's total; the count and total at the top cover every match, not just what's loaded.
  • Paging: 50 at a time, "Load older" fetches the next page by date cursor (new before parameter); filters live in the URL.
  • Dashed tags are sources, context only and not a filter: a purchase confirmed by a statement shows both chat and statement.
  • One row: the search (merchant and item names) and a Filters button with the count of filters on. The panel holds Period (All time by default, the same choices as Reports), Privacy, Category and For; changes apply as you go, and "Show 12 purchases" closes it. On a phone the panel is a sheet from the bottom.
  • Each filter on shows as a chip under the search; × removes it, "Clear all" removes them all.
  • New API filters: private, q, before.
  • A purchase logged by a recurring bill has a "↻ recurring" tag linking to it on Recurring. The row itself opens the purchase: its link is the merchant name, stretched over the row, so the tag can be a link of its own.
  • Category and For pick several, as checkbox lists in the panel. Three-state: ticking a parent ticks it and all its subcategories; unticking one (Alcohol) turns the parent into a dash, meaning the parent's own purchases and the subcategories still ticked ("Groceries except Alcohol"). A fully ticked parent sends just the parent, so later subcategories are included; a dashed one sends the parent alone (only=true) plus each ticked subcategory, so a subcategory added later is left out until ticked. The example has Eating out, and Groceries without Alcohol: click Filters to see it. "Nobody in particular" finds purchases with no person or thing. The API takes repeated category and cost_center parameters.
