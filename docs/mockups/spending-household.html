Design notes

  • The same page as My spending over scope=household: every member's non-private purchases.
  • No chart or table splits by member; that rule is the ledger's, so the API doesn't offer it either (VISION.md §4.A).
  • Private purchases are left out entirely, including from top merchants.
  • No watched groups and no caps here: both are personal, so they live on My spending.
  • Top merchants only lists merchants with purchases from at least two members, or a household-wide total above a floor, so one member's single private-ish habit isn't singled out.
