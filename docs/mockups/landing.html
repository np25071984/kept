How it works

Say it, snap it, forward it

A sentence, a receipt photo, an order email or a bank statement. Your assistant reads it; Kept files it once, even when it arrives three ways.

One question, a good guess

"Groceries, like last Saturday?" Kept tells your assistant when to ask, when to stay quiet, and always to confirm before saving.

See where it goes

Per car, per kid, per month. Ask in the chat, or review and fix everything on the web.

Your data

  • Kept never connects to your bank or inbox.
    Your assistant reads what you show it; Kept keeps what was logged, not the files.
  • Your purchases are yours.
    Others in your household see shared totals, never your list. Private purchases stay out of totals too.

Design notes

  • English or Russian from the browser's language; the header link switches to the other one and remembers the choice in a cookie that only stores the language.
  • Served at / for anyone signed out; a signed-in visitor goes straight to My purchases. The header's "Sign in" is the only way in from here.
  • Answers what Kept is, what happens to the data and which assistants it works with (VISION.md §3.4, §4.A "Households, Accounts and Privacy").
  • The chat is the product, so a phone-sized conversation sits beside the headline: a messenger's wallpaper, bubbles and times. "Your assistant" names no product. Its lines follow the playbook: best guess as a yes/no question, confirm, one-line result. On a phone it stacks under the headline.
  • No connection steps or MCP address here: those come after sign-in, on Settings → Assistants & keys and after creating a household.
  • Static HTML, no tracking or cookies. The privacy policy is its own page, linked from here and from the sign-in pages.
  • Not live until sign-up opens to other households (ROADMAP.md, Later).
