Design notes

  • The household is what members share: who is in it, its categories, its people & things, and its settings. Notes, watched groups and caps are personal: Preferences and Settings → Assistant notes (VISION.md §4.A).
  • Tabs keep each part short; the tab is in the URL (/settings/household/members), so links and the back button work.
  • Owners see the buttons; members see the list.
  • Removing a member revokes their access at once (sign-ins and keys) and leaves their purchases out of household totals from then on. The last owner can't be removed or demoted.
  • New endpoints: GET/DELETE /api/v1/members, PATCH /api/v1/members/{id} (role), GET /api/v1/invites.
