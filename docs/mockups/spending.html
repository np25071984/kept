Design notes

  • One dropdown sets the period; the dates it covers show beside it. "Custom…" adds From and To fields in their place. The choice is in the URL.
  • One question per chart, one data colour; the previous period is a muted tick, never a second colour (no dual axes, no stacks).
  • While a period runs, comparisons use the same days of the previous one ("Sep 1–29"), so a half month isn't compared with a whole one.
  • The running month is hatched and labelled "so far"; the dashed line is the 12-month average.
  • Categories roll up into their parents, with subcategories listed under them; a click filters My purchases.
  • A cap shows here only from 80%, one line each, linking to Preferences, where caps are set (VISION.md §4.A, "Caps").
  • Watched groups (yours) show the amount found, how much of the spending where they're bought was checked, and the estimate for the rest (VISION.md §4.A, "Coverage"). Low coverage reads as "about".
  • Every chart has a hover title per mark and "Show as table". Data: GET /api/v1/expenses/summary with scope=me.
  • On a phone the tiles stack and the month chart shows the last 6 months, so its labels stay readable.
